Scoring is now yours to set.

■ Set your own pass line
Steps and exercise each get a pass line and a goal line. The pass line is worth 40 points, the goal line 60. A day that reaches both pass lines scores 80 — a pass.
So "10,000 steps and two exercises is a good day" goes in exactly as you would say it.
Settings now shows a score table in 1,000-step rows. Move a line and the table redraws, so you can see what your days will score before you commit.
The old scoring modes (balanced, steps first, exercise first) have been removed.

■ Choose the day you started
Set the day you began keeping the diary. Days before it turn dark grey and stay out of your scores and pass rate.
This matters if you moved to a new phone and Health carried over months of old step data.

■ Fixed: step count not refreshing
If you left the app open past midnight, the calendar kept yesterday as "today". Steps were also not re-read when you came back from another app.
Both now refresh when the date changes and whenever you return to the app.

■ About past scores
Changing the pass lines does not move scores on days already settled (older than three days), so your history stays put. If you do want past days recalculated, there is a button for it under Settings → Scoring.

Note: this update resets the step settings to their defaults (pass 10,000, goal 16,000), because the way scores are calculated has changed. Your records are kept.
